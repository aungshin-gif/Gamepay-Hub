Privacy Policy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. What we collect

We only collect what's needed to process your order and support your account:

DataWhy
Email addressLogin, order confirmations, password reset codes
Order detailsProduct, plan, amount, payment method, and the account/contact you're topping up
Payment screenshotProof of payment, so an admin can approve your order
Chat messagesSupport conversation about your specific order
Chosen display name / avatarShown back to you in your own account and chat — cosmetic only

We don't collect payment card numbers or bank login details — payment happens entirely inside your own KBZPay/WavePay/etc. app; we only ever see the screenshot you choose to upload as proof.

2. Who else sees it

Two service providers process data on our behalf, strictly to run the store — neither has permission to use your data for anything else:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, login system, and payment-slip storage.
  • Resend — sends account emails (signup confirmation, password reset) on our behalf.

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

3. How long we keep it

Order and chat history is kept indefinitely so you can always see your own order history and so we can resolve any dispute about a past order. You can ask us to delete your account at any time (see Contact below) — this removes your login, but past order records are kept for our own bookkeeping, with personal details minimized where possible.

4. Your choices

  • You can browse and buy as a guest without creating an account — in that case we only keep what's needed for that one order (tied to a private order link, not a login).
  • If you do create an account, you can change your display name, username, avatar, and password at any time from "My Account".

5. Security

Your account data sits behind Supabase's access controls (row-level security), so only you and store admins can see your own orders and messages — not other customers. As with any online service, no method of storage is guaranteed 100% secure, but we don't store anything beyond what's listed above.

6. Contact

Questions about your data, or want it deleted? Reach us on Telegram at @angsthtun.

This page is a general policy written for GamePay Hub's own use and isn't formal legal advice. If you need certainty about Myanmar-specific data-protection requirements, it's worth having a local lawyer review this before relying on it.
