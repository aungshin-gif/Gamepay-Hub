Refund Policy

When you can get a refund, and when you can't.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

The short version

Every product on GamePay Hub is a digital good delivered directly to your account — a subscription login, a top-up code, or similar. Like most digital-goods stores, once a working product has actually been delivered to you and you've had the chance to use it, the sale is final. Before that point, you're covered.

✓ Refundable

  • Order still "Pending" and you want to cancel before it's approved
  • We approved the order but haven't delivered yet, and there's an unreasonable delay
  • What was delivered doesn't match what you paid for (wrong plan/product)
  • A delivered login/code simply doesn't work, and we can't fix or replace it

✗ Not refundable

  • The product was delivered correctly and works as described
  • You changed your mind after a working account/code was delivered
  • The account was suspended by the original provider (e.g. Netflix, OpenAI) for violating their own terms — not something GamePay Hub controls
  • The payment screenshot submitted was fake, edited, or reused (see Terms of Service)

How to request a refund

  1. Open the order's chat (from "My Orders" → the order → Chat) and explain the issue, or message us directly on Telegram.
  2. Include your order code (looks like GPH-20260902-01123) so we can find it quickly.
  3. We'll review and reply — most refund decisions are made within the same day.
  4. Approved refunds are sent back to the same payment method you used, and usually take 1-3 business days to appear.

Contact

Reach us on Telegram at @angsthtun for any refund request or question.

This page is a general policy written for GamePay Hub's own use and isn't formal legal advice. If you need certainty about consumer-protection requirements in Myanmar, it's worth having a local lawyer review this before relying on it.
